## Operating sequence

1. Verify clean exact-SHA unit CI and archive the successor certificate.
2. An independent human reviews the TIDEWATER packet without performance,
   completes the review form and seals a new record. Confirmation permits a
   separate human decision on the unchanged evaluation declaration. A reopen
   decision does not authorize fallback admission.
3. Continue bounded official discovery, measuring repairs after every batch.
   BSE requires archived reviewed cross-listing proof; issuer routes require
   reviewed host ownership. Unavailable routes stay recorded as unavailable.
4. Review material capital facts and source inventories, verify events, build
   reconciled share histories and certify a shared filing/share interval.
5. Seal unchanged V1 PRICE and TOTAL_RETURN evidence. Review and freeze full V2
   only after data certification, then run the four arms and all capital cases.
6. Register and release passing sealed candidates. Launch matched cohorts and
   collect the required real sample before a manual promotion decision.

Use the commands in [README.md](README.md). Pending forms must be completed by
the named human; an automated agent must not fill an identity or approve them.
